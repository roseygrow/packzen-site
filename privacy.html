Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

The short version

PackZen has no accounts and no advertising, and it does not track you. Your trips and packing lists stay on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. We cannot see them. The only information that reaches a service of ours is your purchase status, described below.

What the app stores, and where

Trips, destinations, dates, packing lists, your stay details and your optional profile name and emoji are stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled for PackZen, the same data syncs through your private iCloud database so it appears on your other devices. That database belongs to your Apple Account; we have no access to it.

You can export your trips to a file or delete them at any time in the app. Deleting the app removes its data from the device; iCloud data can be removed in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage.

What is sent to Apple on your behalf

Weather. To show a forecast, the app sends the coordinates of your trip's destination city — not your own location — to Apple Weather (WeatherKit).

City search. The text you type when searching for a destination is sent to Apple Maps to find matching places. PackZen never asks for your location.

Purchases. Payments are handled entirely by the App Store; we never see your payment details. To know whether PackZen Pro is active and to understand how subscriptions perform, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your purchase history for PackZen (product, dates, price, country of the storefront) together with a random identifier created for this app. That identifier is not linked to your name, email or Apple Account, and it is not used to track you across other apps or websites. RevenueCat keeps this record for as long as PackZen is offered; to have it deleted, write to the support address below and we will remove it within 30 days.

On-device suggestions. On devices with Apple Intelligence, optional item suggestions are generated on the device itself. Your text is not sent to us or to any third party.

Apple's handling of this information is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Checkout reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent from a server and do not require sharing anything with us.

Measuring our own advertising

We advertise PackZen on TikTok and Meta. To know whether an ad led to an install, the app (from version 1.1) uses AppsFlyer together with Apple's SKAdNetwork, which reports installs to an ad network without identifying the person. AppsFlyer receives the identifier Apple assigns to this app on your device (not the advertising identifier — we never ask for permission to track, and the advertising identifier is not collected) and four anonymous milestones: a trip was created, a list was completed, the upgrade screen was shown, a room check was completed. No trip details, dates or items are sent. Purchases are reported to AppsFlyer by RevenueCat so that we can see which ads pay for themselves. None of this is linked to your name or Apple Account, and it is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What we do not do

The app shows no ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or share personal data. Apart from the purchase and measurement information described above, it collects nothing.

Children

PackZen is a general-audience travel tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
